NOTES

Diffusion field — eight loudspeakers on an ellipse. The cursor is a virtual source; gains are computed with DBAP (distance-based amplitude panning) and drawn as dot size and line opacity. Move the cursor, or leave it and the source drifts.

Wave field synthesis — a linear array reconstructs a wavefront from a source behind it. Each speaker fires when the primary wavefront reaches it, and the superposition of those wavelets rebuilds the front. This is Huygens' principle, which is what WFS rests on.
