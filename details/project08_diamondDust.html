XR with AI Character

Diamond Dust

Year: 2024 Role: Music Composer Venue: Ars Electronica 2024

WOVEN ECHOES: A VIRTUAL TAPESTRY OF HUMANITY

Diamond Dust offers a unique experience with AI characters and advanced haptics. As you crash-land in an ice cave on a snowy mountain, you meet Kardi, an ice fairy. Engaging in heartfelt conversations with Kardi reveals the value of cherished relationships.

Participating deeply in these discussions allows you to form a special friendship with an AI. Experience this unique blend of art and technology by sharing your true feelings.

Constraints

Diamond Dust travelled as an XR piece with a head-mounted display, and no loudspeaker array was available at the venues it was shown in. The spatial layer was therefore built entirely in Unity and delivered over headphones.

The authoring was the same either way — a source is given a position in the scene and moved through it. What changed was what arrived at the listener. Head tracking held the sound field steady as a visitor turned, which an array cannot do for one person in a crowd; but the physical excitation of a room was gone, and each visitor heard a generic HRTF rather than the space they were standing in.
